# Help with Tunelyst

## Getting started

Download the latest APK from [Releases](https://github.com/nyekeza/Tunelyst/releases/latest) and open it on your phone. You may need to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. Tunelyst needs Android 10 or later.

Open Tunelyst and allow access to your music when asked. Your songs need to be stored on your phone; music from streaming apps may not be available to Tunelyst.

## I can't see my songs

- Check that the songs are saved on your phone and can be played by another app.
- Check Tunelyst's music permission in your phone's app settings.
- Give your phone a little time to find newly added music, then reopen Tunelyst.

If a song still doesn't appear or won't play, [let us know](https://github.com/nyekeza/Tunelyst/issues/new/choose).

## Updating Tunelyst

Download the latest APK from Releases and open it to install the update. If Android refuses to update an older copy, it may have been signed with a different key. Removing that copy can erase its playlists and settings, so check and preserve anything important before uninstalling.

## Need more help?

[Report a problem](https://github.com/nyekeza/Tunelyst/issues/new/choose) and tell us what happened, your phone model, and Android version. Please don't post passwords or other private information.
