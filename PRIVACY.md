# Privacy

This note describes Tunelyst 1.0.0.

Tunelyst asks for access to music on your device so it can show and play your songs. Your playlists, liked songs, listening history, and settings are kept on your device. Features such as Smart Shuffle use your local listening activity.

You can change music access in your phone's app settings. Removing the app or clearing its data can delete its local playlists, history, and settings. Your music files are separate, but check your phone's prompts before deleting anything.

If you choose to share or export information, it goes to the app or location you select. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone, so please don't include passwords, private files, or personal details in them.
